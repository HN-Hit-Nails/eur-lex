Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 7 September 2026, the European Commission received notification of a
        proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
        Platinum Equity, LLC (together with its affiliates ‘Platinum Equity Group’,
        United States), and Nestlé S.A. (‘Nestlé’, Switzerland) (together the “Parties”)
        will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger
        Regulation joint control of Nestlé Waters (Luxembourg) (3) by way of purchase
        of shares. Nestlé Waters is currently wholly owned and solely controlled by
        Nestlé. (4)



( 1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
       on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
       replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
       terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
( 2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
( 3)   A special purpose vehicle company, incorporated for this Transaction ‘WPB JVco S.à r.l. ‘ and
       based in Luxembourg, holds Nestlé Group’s waters and beverages business (‘Nestlé Waters’).
( 4)   OJ C, C/4931, 23.9.2026.
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      Platinum Equity Group globally specializes in the merger, acquisition and
               operation of companies that provide services and solutions to customers in
               a broad range of businesses, including information technology,
               telecommunications, logistics, metal services, manufacturing and
               distribution,
        –      Nestlé is primarily involved in the production, marketing, and sale of a large
               variety of food and beverage products globally, including dairy products,
               coffee beverages, packaged water, cereals, culinary products including
               prepared food, condiments, sauces, and dry pasta, ice cream, chocolate and
               tea beverages, confectionery products, snacks, pet food, and healthcare
               nutrition products.
(3)     The business activities of Nestlé Waters are the production and global supply of
        bottled or packaged water and beverages, comprising still, sparkling, and
        flavoured water, as well as non-alcoholic beverages, including flavoured
        sparkling beverages.
(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (5)
(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
