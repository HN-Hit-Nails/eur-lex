Explanatory note
Cross-regulatory cooperation between data protection supervisory authorities (DPAs) and
other national and EU competent authorities has become increasingly important as legal and
practical challenges emerge at the intersection of different regulatory fields. Cooperation
agreements can be an important basis to organise and strengthen cooperation and dialogue
between authorities across regulatory fields.
To support this, the EDPB developed a Template for Cross-Regulatory Cooperation
Agreements, drawing on agreements already concluded by DPAs with other competent
authorities and work done by the former EDPB Taskforce on the interplay between data
protection, competition and consumer protection law.
This Template aims to provide DPAs with a flexible basis for negotiating cross-regulatory
cooperation agreements, irrespective of their form. It should not be understood as binding
instructions limiting how cross-regulatory cooperation agreements ought to be drafted. Rather,
it is intended as a point of reference for negotiations between competent authorities that can
be adjusted according to different needs. The Template may also be used to inform
amendments of existing cooperation agreements.
In order to be adaptable to the specificities of all EEA Member States, the Template remains
general in some areas and leaves certain provisions to be completed by the Signatories. An
effective cooperation agreement would supplement the Template with specific references to
EU or Member State law circumscribing the mandates and competences of the Signatories,
so that the agreement can be specifically suited to the cooperation between the competent
authorities, taking into account their specific competences, the context, and, in particular, the
legal framework in which they operate. Furthermore, the provisions in the Template do not
need to be viewed as a bundle. Signatories might select only those provisions that they
consider fitting or necessary for their specific cooperation context.
This Template seeks to contribute to the EDPB’s strategic objective of safeguarding data
protection in the developing digital and cross-regulatory landscape by providing resources to
support the cooperation between DPAs and other competent authorities. 1 It also seeks to
promote best practices as regards cross-regulatory governance and cooperation, an objective
underlined during the recent Helsinki high-level meeting. More specifically, this Template
pursues the commitment in the Helsinki Statement to foster “structured cooperation with other
regulators to share experiences and to address legal and practical challenges to cross-
regulatory cooperation on concrete cases”. 2

CHAPTER III DATA PROTECTION AND CONFIDENTIALITY
Article 14 Processing of personal data
(1) The Signatories will process the personal data related to the performance of this agreement
in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the ‘GDPR’) [and/or Regulation (EU)
2018/1725, as applicable] and other applicable laws.
(2) Within the scope of this agreement, the legal basis for the processing of personal data is
Article 6(1)(e) of the GDPR.
(3) The Signatories will, in principle, determine the purposes and means of the processing
separately, pursuant to the execution of their individual competences and mandates. To the
extent that the Signatories jointly determine the purposes and means of the processing, 26 they
agree to determine their respective responsibilities for compliance with the GDPR pursuant to

(1) All information exchanged pursuant to this Agreement will be treated as confidential unless
specified otherwise. Information exchanged will be marked with an appropriate security
